## Production corrections

The first prop renders contained painted checkerboards rather than alpha. They were rejected. Built-in image edits removed backgrounds; metadata was checked before encoding. The accepted source filenames are recorded in `sources.json`. The final blue van was derived from the accepted transparent red van for consistent silhouette and scale.

Successful cutout prompts used the specific subject, for example: “Remove the background from the gold bag. Return a transparent PNG cutout with an alpha channel.” The vault correction explicitly requested alpha=0 outside the object and preservation of its materials and shape. Crook cutouts preserved all four views and their placement.

Blue-van derivation: “Create the opposing blue-team version of this transparent game sprite. Change the red paint to cobalt blue and horizontally mirror the van so its open rear ramp faces down-left. Keep the same miniature toy materials and warm upper-left lighting. Keep the background fully transparent in the PNG alpha channel. No checkerboard, ground, text or new objects.”

The courtyard correction removed foreground walls, lamps, greenery and fake loot; widened the clear floor; and confined architecture to the upper edge. The host compresses its 210px facade band into the non-playable 172px header band.

`encode.cjs` performs deterministic alpha-bound normalization, sprite-cell sizing and WebP encoding (quality88, alpha quality100). It does not create or paint artwork. Invoke from repository root with an asset name and its source PNG path. Original PNGs remain in the archive. All eight runtime files together are approximately370KiB; seven carry genuine alpha, and the courtyard is intentionally opaque.

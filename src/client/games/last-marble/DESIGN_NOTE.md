# Last Marble tile floor

Eight seeded boundary-erosion waves remove 25 cream tiles in groups of three
(four in the last wave). A three-second warning begins at 2 seconds and repeats
every five seconds, so the room gets a constant reaction window while the floor
steadily narrows. The remaining grid stays four-neighbour connected; threatened
tiles have a route of at most three 100-unit steps to surviving floor.

Phones say “tiles” because the projector is the only place players need to read
which group is about to go. The controller remains a one-thumbstick game: build
momentum, ram rivals, and stay on the remaining floor.

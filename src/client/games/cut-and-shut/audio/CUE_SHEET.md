# Cut & Shut cue sheet

The procedural Web Audio layer is the production source for this geometry-first slice: it is offline, tiny, deterministic and needs no credentials. Every cue is reinforced visually and the game remains complete when muted or autoplay is blocked. No music is planned because live bargaining is the intended room soundtrack.

| cue | meaning | sound design | length | voices | gain / priority | ducking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `offer` | a public offer opens | dry paper flick plus quiet click | 0.12s one-shot | 1 | 0.07 / low | none |
| `stitch` | a swap is accepted or road locks | two metallic clacks, rising | 0.22s one-shot | 2 | 0.12 / high | none |
| `fold` | adjacency changes | short descending mechanical groan | 0.48s one-shot | 1 | 0.12 / critical | clears low cues |
| `step` | one of exactly six courier beats | compact clock escapement tick | 0.08s one-shot | 1 | 0.08 / high | none |
| `fail` | courier enters canal | soft low drop, never startling | 0.28s one-shot | 1 | 0.1 / high | none |
| `result` | market/final score lands | wonky three-note council stamp | 0.45s one-shot | 3 | 0.12 / high | none |

Polyphony is capped at four sources per surface. Host cues are shared; accepted/rejected/commit phone feedback is haptic and textual so ten handsets do not create an uncontrolled room mix.

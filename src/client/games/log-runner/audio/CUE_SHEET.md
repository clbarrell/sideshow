# Log Runner cue sheet

Every critical cue is currently rendered with the committed offline procedural Web Audio mix. The sample filenames below are reserved for a later room-mix pass; the grey-box build does not request them, and remains complete if autoplay is blocked or the party is muted.

| cue / file | gameplay meaning | generation prompt | length | max voices | gain / priority | ducking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `countdown.mp3` | final hands-ready beats | Dry hollow woodblock tick, park broadcast style, no reverb, no voice | 0.12s one-shot | 1 | 0.14 / high | none |
| `go.mp3` | shared start | Two quick rising wooden marimba notes with a crisp river-stone click, upbeat, no voice | 0.35s one-shot | 1 | 0.20 / critical | lower warning bus 3dB |
| `warning.mp3` | a real obstacle enters the warning lane | Single muted log knock with a short airy rush, readable and calm, not alarming | 0.25s one-shot | 1 | 0.12 / high | none |
| `jump.mp3` | local player jumps | Tiny taut spring and wooden tap, playful, dry, very short | 0.16s one-shot | 2 | 0.10 phone / medium | none |

No music is planned. Spoken calls, wrong calls, laughter and the cadence of warning knocks are the desired soundtrack; a continuous bed would mask those social cues. One draft and at most one targeted regeneration per rejected cue is the production limit.

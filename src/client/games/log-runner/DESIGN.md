# Log Runner concept and audit remedy

Log Runner — stay on the shared log longest, then heckle from the bank.

The moment is a whole line of lumberjacks choosing together, with the wrong call producing a group splash. Free-for-all survival; one fixed perspective camera shows every player. Landscape phones retain two large independent thumb buttons, JUMP and DUCK. The first tap locks the one current shared obstacle; repeat and correction taps are ignored until it resolves. A persistent projector marker and matching phone message expose the accepted answer. No private answer queue remains.

The nine-second launch runway mounts controls and teaches the objective. Ten seconds of explicitly unscored practice follow, with one low and one high obstacle and equal safety for everyone. A second GO starts competitive scoring. One point per completed survival second, plus 15/10/5 place bonuses; ties share place. Practice never changes survival rankings. The full session remains bounded by 90 live seconds plus runway/result hold (80 scored seconds).

Every obstacle, including harmless spray and bank branches, gets a full 2.4-second actionable warning. A fixed 150ms late grace remains. Phrases keep variety, but their steps are retimed at least 2.8 seconds apart. Longer between-phrase rests provide full isolated bank slots. Escalation changes composition and density, never warning duration. Fakes are explicitly labelled spray and require no answer.

Eliminated players move to a visible bank roster with a one-button heckle, personal nine-second cooldown, fair round-robin admission, one active bank branch and eight-second global spacing. A branch miss produces an attributed stumble, no points or knockouts. Branch warnings cannot consume answers intended for another obstacle or shorten its warning; comic distraction can still indirectly affect a person's attention. This role's ability to sustain interest for an early-out player remains a human-playtest hypothesis.

All ten identities have names, numbers, seat colours and varied hat silhouettes. Names stay stationary under responsive characters. Local audio, bounded particle work and reduced-motion behavior preserve timing and readability; mute does not remove information. Unknown input/replays are ignored. Reconnect restores role, shared target, locked answer and cooldown. Late arrivals join the bank.

Build cost: moderate visual work within the existing Canvas2D seam; no shell or Durable Object changes. Target: 16.7ms p95 CPU draw/update and approximately 60fps at 1920×1080 on the actual browser, ten players, repeated cycles. Automated canvas mocks are behavior checks, not GPU/performance proof.

Acceptance: every phone and projector target agree before and after repeated taps; each action has its entire warning; practice treats identical mistakes identically; bank throws remain isolated; scoring and role consequences are visible. Real participants must still validate no-spoken comprehension, shared-room timing, bank participation, visual appeal and room audio mix.

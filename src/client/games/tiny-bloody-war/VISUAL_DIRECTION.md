# Tiny Bloody War — visual direction

The two approved mockups in `docs/concepts/tiny-bloody-war/` are direction, not runtime assets. Runtime art uses resolution-independent canvas primitives for stable tinting, animation and silhouette clarity.

- **Palette:** warm parchment sand, charcoal ink, rust-red squares, electric teal triangles, ivory type.
- **Shape:** soldiers are broad helmet-and-shield circles; archers are narrow hooded diamonds with long bows. Team shape and numbered seat medallions reinforce colour.
- **Texture:** sparse stipple, cracks and low stone cover; combat space stays quiet.
- **Motion:** readable prepare/commit/recover poses, short paint flecks, block sparks, knockback, squash on return and a restrained victory banner. Reduced motion removes flecks and pose overshoot.
- **Type:** local bold sans-serif with dark outline; HUD occupies one narrow band.

# Borderline visual direction

- Midnight navy sea, warm cream paper, and the existing ten seat colours.
- A native-canvas 6×4 cut-paper atlas keeps province numbers aligned with the phone grid.
- Every faction combines its seat colour, name and a distinct emblem. Ten home-port marks remain outside capturable land; each score row names both permanent entry provinces.
- Reveal orders originate at the player's permanent port emblem. Temporary numbered force tokens ride oversized arrows; guards appear as temporary rings. No standing-army piles imply movement from a province.
- All orders animate in parallel. Flags switch together at the shared capture beat, followed by a score-bank recap. Reduced motion shows the same complete arrows and static outcomes without travel or pulsing.

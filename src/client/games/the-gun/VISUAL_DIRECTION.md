# The Gun visual direction

**Idea:** a midnight rooftop construction site staged as a fluorescent public-safety poster. It should read first as a competitive diagram, then reward a closer look with stamped concrete texture and chunky industrial motion.

- **Palette:** ink navy `#071820`, night teal `#0D2A32`, concrete cream `#F4E9D4`, hazard amber `#FFC247`, signal red `#FF5A47`, electric cyan `#5DE4E7`, plus player seat colours. Amber is reserved for incoming supply; cyan identifies the live weapon; red means lethal line or death.
- **Identity:** every fighter carries name, seat number, one of ten glyphs, a seat-colour body, and a different hard-hat/torso motif. Facing is explicit through visor and nose direction.
- **Gun state:** the holder gets a tall spotlight, `THE GUN` crown, weapon silhouette, and a labelled reload ring. The whole roster rail marks the holder. The gun on the ground pulses inside a cyan pickup bracket.
- **Threat language:** supply targets use a striped amber landing zone, vertical cable, countdown, and `GUN IN N` label. The shot is a thick white-red tracer with a muzzle flash and impact cross, held long enough to parse but never obscuring movement.
- **Type:** local heavy system/Archivo stack. Timer and state callout are largest, player tags second, details third. No remote fonts.
- **Texture:** code-native diagonal scaffold braces, concrete chips, safety stripes, and distant windows. Texture stays outside player silhouettes, tags, and warning zones.
- **Motion:** controlled anticipation—warning beacon, cable drop, pickup shockwave, recoil, shove arc, short hit-stop impression, bounded particles, respawn beam. Camera shake is brief and disabled under reduced motion.
- **Native rendering:** canvas and CSS are the production assets because crisp dynamic identity, arbitrary scaling, and reduced-motion support matter more than painterly decoration.

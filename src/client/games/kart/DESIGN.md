# Backyard Circuit

**Promise:** anyone can hold GO and steer through their first corner, while a practiced player can hold a fast turn to load a controllable slide and release or countersteer for a small exit boost.

The shout-out-loud moment is a racer hanging the rear out through a bend, catching it cleanly, and surging past a friend on the exit. Drift charge only builds during sustained fast road turns. Grass, reversing, an active turbo, and rapid left-right wiggling clear or delay it, so the reward comes from committing to a corner rather than farming the stick.

Each seed deterministically selects and reshapes an oval, kidney, peanut, or S-bend course. Courses use 240 evenly spaced samples, ordered checkpoints, bounded curvature, and enough clearance that separate road branches cannot overlap. Figure-eight crossings are deliberately excluded: they need branch-aware collision and checkpoint layers to remain fair and readable with ten cars.

The phone remains glance-light: steering, throttle, and the existing comeback boost are the complete control set. On the projector, the car body and shadow communicate its physical footprint, a quiet exhaust glow shows boost readiness, and the heading pennant fades after GO. A bright yellow arrow appears only when a racer misses the next gate.

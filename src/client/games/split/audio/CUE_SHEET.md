# Split cue sheet

The local MP3 files are optional enhancements. Every critical cue has an offline procedural Web Audio fallback, so a missing file, decode failure, muted device, or blocked autoplay cannot stop the round.

| `ko.mp3` | a border pulse knocks out a survivor | Soft bass impact with brief coral noise crack, celebratory not violent | 0.35s one-shot | 2 | 0.2 / critical | low sine drop |

No music is planned for the grey-box vertical slice. Room negotiation is the soundtrack, and a continuous bed would compete with the shouted bluffing the game is meant to produce. Repeated countdown ticks are capped; state remains fully visible when muted.
